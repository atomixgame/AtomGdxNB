# Development Progress: AtomGdx Studio v0.1.73

## Implementation Timeline & Changelog

### Version v0.1.73 (Latest)
- **Separated 3D Components (Viewer vs Editor)**:
  - **3D Model Viewer** (`Model3DViewerTopComponent` / `Model3DViewerPanel`): Read-Only model viewer featuring quick-switching for 10 popular GLTF models, orbit controls, and a prominent yellow `"Read-Only Asset Preview"` banner in the Inspector.
  - **3D Scene Editor** (`Scene3DEditorTopComponent` / `Scene3DEditorPanel`): Full interactive scene editor with Transform Gizmo Modes (*Select Q, Move W, Rotate E, Scale R*), Shading options (*PBR Shaded, Wireframe, Unlit*), and View Snapping (*Top, Front, Right, Iso, Reset*).
- **Unity-Inspired 3D View Orientation Gizmo**:
  - Rendered interactive 3D Orientation Gizmo in the viewport corner with projected colored axes (*Red X, Green Y, Blue Z*) and central isometric cube.
- **3D SceneGraph Icons & Context Menus**:
  - Upgraded `SceneGraphTreePanel` with custom icons for meshes, lights, cameras, prefabs, and particles.
  - Comprehensive Context Menu: `+ Add Child` (Primitives, Lights, Camera, Prefabs, Empty), `Duplicate (Ctrl+D)`, `Toggle Visibility (H)`, `Rename (F2)`, and `Delete (Del)`.
- **10 Popular GLTF Demo Models**:
  - Added 10 complete GLTF models in `Workspace/NeonCosmos/assets/models/`: `spacecraft_cruiser.gltf`, `asteroid_large.gltf`, `scifi_turret.gltf`, `cargo_container.gltf`, `energy_shield_bubble.gltf`, `space_station_module.gltf`, `cyber_hovercraft.gltf`, `quantum_warp_beacon.gltf`, `modular_scifi_wall.gltf`, `plasma_cannon_heavy.gltf`.
  - Upgraded `Model3DViewportListener` to construct composite multi-mesh geometries (hulls, wings, engines, solar arrays, turrets) with PBR materials.
- **Visual Verification Proofs**:
  - `model3d_opengl_gpu_proof.png`: Verified native LibGDX 3D OpenGL hardware rendering of composite multi-mesh spacecraft and Unity orientation gizmo.
  - `scenegraph_3d_palette_proof.png`: Verified docked layout of SceneGraph 3D tree, 3D Scene Editor, Read-Only Inspector with metadata, and 56-item Asset Palette.

### Version v0.1.72
- 3D SceneGraph & Palette integration with `palette_items.json`.
- Full-width edge-to-edge canvas with stats migrated to Inspector.
